## 🔪 Fear Street Trilogy
A terrifying, interconnected slasher horror experience based on R.L. Stine's books, spanning multiple time periods in the cursed town of Shadyside.

### Chronological Breakdown:
1. **Fear Street Part One: 1994** — A group of teens stumble upon an ancient evil.
2. **Fear Street Part Two: 1978** — A bloody summer at Camp Nightwing.
3. **Fear Street Part Three: 1666** — Uncovering the true origin of the curse.
